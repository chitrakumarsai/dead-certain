# Library Fine Rules for __APP_TITLE__

This is a fictional notice written as a starter for Dead Certain. It is not a real library's policy. Amounts are in cents.

## 1. Daily rate

A late book is fined for each full day it is late. The rate depends on the kind of member:

- Child member: 10 cents a day.
- Adult member: 25 cents a day.
- Senior member: 15 cents a day.

## 2. Cap on the fine

The fine for one book never goes above 500 cents for a child member and 1000 cents for any other member.

## 3. Waiver card

A member who holds a fine waiver card pays no fine. The card cancels the fine that is left after the cap.

## 4. Result

A book returned on time carries no fine. A late book carries the fine from clauses 1 and 2, unless a waiver card cancels it under clause 3. The fine to pay is 0 for a book returned on time, and for a late book it is the fine after the cap less the amount a waiver card cancels.
