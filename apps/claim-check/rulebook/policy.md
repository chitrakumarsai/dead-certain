# Sample Health Policy

This is a fictional policy written for Dead Certain. It is not a real insurance product, and it does not follow any insurer's wording. All amounts are in Indian rupees (₹).

## 1. Basis of payment

### 1.1 Words used in this policy

- **Policyholder**: the person this policy covers and who makes the claim.
- **Hospital bill**: the total on the hospital's final bill for one admission.
- **Policy year**: the 12 months that start on the policy start date.
- **Age**: the policyholder's age in completed years on the day of admission.
- **Illness start date**: the date of the first symptoms or of the first diagnosis, whichever is earlier.
- **Accident**: a sudden injury from an outside cause, such as a fall or a road collision.
- **Room rent**: the daily charge the hospital makes for the room and bed.

### 1.2 What the policy pays

The policy pays the hospital bill less every deduction in this policy. It never pays more than the hospital bill.

### 1.3 Order of calculation

The policy works out a claim in these steps, in this order.

1. If clause 3.1, 5.1 or 5.2 applies, the whole claim is not paid. The deduction is the full hospital bill, and steps 2 to 5 are skipped.
2. Take off the Annexure 1 items (clause 5.3).
3. Apply the room rent limit (clause 4.2).
4. Take the co-payment (clause 6.1) from the amount left after steps 2 and 3.
5. Apply the sum insured limit (clauses 2.2 and 2.3) to the amount left after step 4.

Every amount worked out under this policy is rounded to the nearest whole rupee. Exactly half a rupee rounds up.

The claim is paid in full when no deduction applies. It is paid in part when the payable amount is more than zero and at least one deduction applies. It is not paid when the payable amount is zero.

Example. The hospital bill is ₹2,10,000, which includes ₹12,000 of Annexure 1 items. The room rent limit takes off ₹33,000, and the co-payment takes off ₹33,000. The payable amount is ₹1,32,000.

## 2. Cover and sum insured

### 2.1 What is covered

The policy covers hospital treatment of an illness or an accident when the policyholder is admitted as an in-patient. A claim is made after the admission and the hospital bill is final.

### 2.2 Sum insured

The sum insured is the most the policy pays in one policy year. The policy schedule shows one of three amounts: ₹3,00,000, ₹5,00,000 or ₹10,00,000.

### 2.3 Claims already paid in the policy year

Every amount the policy has already paid in the same policy year counts against the sum insured. The amount left for a new claim is the sum insured less those payments, and never less than zero. If the amount left after the co-payment is higher than this, the policy deducts the difference.

## 3. Waiting period

### 3.1 Waiting period

An illness is not covered if its start date falls less than 30 days after the policy start date. An illness that starts 30 or more days after the policy start date is covered. The waiting period does not apply to an accident, which is covered from the first day of the policy.

## 4. Hospital charges

### 4.1 Room charges and other charges

Room charges are the room rent for each day multiplied by the number of days the hospital charged for the room. Other charges are the hospital bill less the room charges and less the Annexure 1 items.

### 4.2 Room rent limit

The room rent limit is 1% of the sum insured for each day. For a sum insured of ₹5,00,000 the limit is ₹5,000 a day.

If the room rent for a day is higher than the limit, the policy deducts the difference for every day charged. Other charges then reduce in the same ratio: the policy pays only the limit divided by the room rent, multiplied by the other charges, and deducts the rest.

For a room rent of ₹6,000 a day, a limit of ₹5,000, 5 days and other charges of ₹1,68,000, the policy deducts ₹5,000 for the room and ₹28,000 for the other charges, which is ₹33,000 in all.

If the room rent is at or below the limit, nothing is deducted under this clause.

## 5. Exclusions

### 5.1 Treatment that is never covered

The policy does not pay for cosmetic surgery, for weight-loss treatment, or for dental treatment unless an accident caused the need for it. A claim for any of these is not paid.

### 5.2 Treatment outside India

The policy does not pay for treatment received outside India. A claim for such treatment is not paid.

### 5.3 Items the policy does not pay

The policy does not pay for consumables listed in Annexure 1 (gloves, masks, gowns and similar). The policy deducts the amount the hospital billed for these items.

## 6. Co-payment

### 6.1 Co-payment

A policyholder aged 61 or over pays 20% of every admissible claim. The admissible claim is the hospital bill after the Annexure 1 items and the room rent limit have been taken off. A policyholder aged 60 or under pays no co-payment.

## 7. Filing a claim

### 7.1 Documents needed

The policyholder sends the final hospital bill, the discharge summary and a photo identity document. The claim cannot be decided without the final bill.

### 7.2 How the decision is reported

The decision states the amount payable. It lists every deduction with its amount and the clause that caused it.

## Annexure 1. Items the policy does not pay

- Surgical gloves and examination gloves
- Face masks and respirators
- Gowns, aprons and drapes
- Head covers and shoe covers
- Disposable bed sheets and pillow covers
- Thermometer covers and probe covers
- Disposable razors and shaving kits
- Hand sanitiser and skin wipes
- Gauze, swabs and cotton wool used for cleaning
- Disposable cups, trays and tableware
